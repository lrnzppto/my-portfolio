---
title: A simple, high-impact website for Don Juan Dancers
summary: A one-page website that gave a well-known Cebu dance group a home for their story and brought in more inquiries.
categories: [web-development]
client: Don Juan Dancers
clientType: Dance group, Cebu, Philippines
role: Website design and build (one-page site)
challenge: >-
  Don Juan, a well-known dance group in Cebu, wanted to grow their social media presence and reach people who had never heard their story.
  They were active in the local scene but had no central online space that clearly introduced who they were, what they offered and why
  they stood out. That made it hard for new followers and potential clients to understand the brand.
approach: >-
  I designed and built a clean, engaging one-page website that shows what Don Juan is all about as you scroll: About Us, the group's
  journey, the services they offer and highlights from their performances. The goal was a site that is simple, modern and easy to
  explore, so any visitor could quickly connect with the group's identity.
outcome: >-
  Within a few months of launch, more people discovered the group through the site, and their story finally had a place where it could
  be seen and shared. Social media engagement increased and they started receiving more inquiries from clients who now understood who
  they were and what they could do.
